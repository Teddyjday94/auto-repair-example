§ 02

How a visit works

Six steps. You approve every dollar at step four.

  1. 01

    Drop off

    Book online or roll in. Keys go in the night box if you're early.

  2. 02

    Inspect

    A tech runs a 47-point check and scans every module, not just the one that's yelling.

  3. 03

    Show & tell

    You get a text with photos, video, and a quote sorted by "now", "soon", and "watch it".

  4. 04

    You decide

    Approve line by line from your phone. Say no to anything. No pressure, no calls.

  5. 05

    Wrench

    OEM-grade parts and live progress updates while we work.

  6. 06

    Road test

    Every car gets driven before it's handed back. Then it's under our 24/24 warranty.
