    § 08

    Straight answers

    Do you work on my make?

    Almost certainly. We service all domestic, Asian, and European makes, plus hybrids and most EVs. We have factory-level scan tools for Toyota, Honda, Ford, GM, Stellantis, VW/Audi, BMW, and Mercedes.

    Will using an independent shop void my warranty?

    No. Federal law (Magnuson-Moss) protects your right to have maintenance done anywhere. We use OEM-spec fluids and parts and log everything, so your records stay clean.

    How does the loaner program work?

    For jobs that run over 4 hours, a loaner is free. You just need a valid license and insurance. Reserve one when you book, since the fleet is first-come.

    What's covered by the 24/24 warranty?

    Parts and labor on every repair for 24 months or 24,000 miles, whichever comes first. It's honored at 30,000+ partner shops nationwide if you're on the road.

    Can I bring my own parts?

    Sometimes. We'll install customer-supplied parts on a case-by-case basis, but we can't warranty the part itself, only our labor.

    Do you offer financing?

    Yes. 6 months no-interest financing on repairs over $500, with approval in about two minutes at the counter or by text.
